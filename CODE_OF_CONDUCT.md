# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Put the safety of users and the project ahead of winning an argument.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `artpar@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
